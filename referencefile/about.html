Our Story & Studio Philosophy

Crafted Slowly Around Warmth, Perfume, and Stillness.

Blooms Flame Wax (BFW) pairs natural plant soy and beeswax with fine botanical fragrance architecture—hand-poured in small batches to light up quiet moments.

Step 01

Perfume Blending

Botanical oils and natural resins are balanced across top, heart, and base notes.

Step 02

Gentle Wax Melt

100% soy and filtered beeswax melt slowly at controlled temperatures for clean combustion.

Step 03

Hand-Poured Vessels

Unbleached cotton wicks are centered by hand inside smoked glass and ceramic jars.

Step 04

14-Day Cure

Every batch rests in dark studio trays so perfume binds deeply throughout the wax.
