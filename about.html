Our story

Poured slowly

A small studio in Kattangudi, Tamil Nadu, turning wax into blooms, keepsakes and festive light.

Since 1999

Made by hand, meant for memories

Blooms & Flame Wax began with a simple belief: a candle should feel personal. Every rose petal, tulip and bubble you see is poured by hand into detailed moulds, set slowly, trimmed and finished in our studio.

From bright terracotta diyas for Deepavali to keepsake couple candles for weddings, we make pieces that are lit on the days that matter.

“A candle should smell like a memory, not a mall.”

The process

From our moulds to your table

We work with our own silicone moulds — like the peony heart beside it — so details stay sharp. Colours are mixed by hand, and each piece is sealed with the BFW label before it leaves the studio.

Custom colours and quantities are available for weddings, return gifts and corporate hampers.
